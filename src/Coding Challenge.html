Coding Challenge

The Concept

What is the BEST Coding Challenge?
A single evening event in Leuven were students from different faculties come together to solve some hard Python challenges and eat free pizza.

Why should you join?
The Coding Challenge is a fun experience that will give you an opportunity to meet new people or deepen friendships, to learn and grow as a programmer. But it is also an opportunity to meet with the great people from companies sponsoring our event who are looking for new talent to hire. There’s prizes for winning teams and free food, so why not give it a try?

Who can participate?
Anyone studying in Leuven is allowed to sign up! You sign up in teams of three people. In order to register we also ask you to upload your CV. Only 80 registration places will be available, so secure your spot quickly!
