About

Who are we?

BEST Leuven is a student organization for engineering students. We organize events and competitions, educational workshops and foreign educational programs. We also prepare social and entertainment events for members throughout the year .

Thanks to BEST, you will gain new contacts, friendships and opportunities across Europe. And most importantly , experience that will complement your studies with practice and help you move forward.
