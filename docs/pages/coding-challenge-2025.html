Coding Challenge 2025

Ready to push your coding skills to the limit? Welcome to the BEST Coding challenge! Whether you’re a seasoned developer or just starting your coding journey, this is your chance to solve imaginative problems, showcase your creativity and outsmart the competition! Code smart, think big and don’t worry, no pilot license required!

The BEST Coding Challenge is an evening where you work together in teams of 3 to solve as many Python challenges as possible. These challenges are fun puzzles around a theme, and they range from beginner-friendly to challenging even for more experienced programmers. Whether you’re new to coding or you have some experience, there’s something to be learned for everyone!

The event takes place on the 25th of November 19:00-22:45 in Quadrivium 01.180. Participation is free, and includes free drinks and food. So gather your friends and mark your calendars, and we hope to see you then!

Registrations for the 2025 Coding Challenge are now closed. Hope to see you again next year!
