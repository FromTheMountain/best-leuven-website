Game Jam 2025

The BEST Leuven Game Jam is here once again to help passionate students take their first step into game development. Learn from industry professionals and put your skills to the test in this 48 hour game dev marathon! It takes place on October 24-26, so save the dates!

What is a game jam?

During a game jam, participants compete against each other, either solo or in teams, to see who can make the best game in a given amount of time. It’s the perfect way to test yourself if you have some experience, and the perfect way to learn if you don’t. At the end, you’ll receive feedback from a panel of judges. And who knows, maybe you can use your jam submission as a base for a full fledged game later…

Why should you participate?

  • Create something wonderful
  • Challenge yourself and compete against others
  • Find other game dev interested students
  • Get in touch with industry professionals
  • Everything is free! (weekend lunch and dinner included)

You can participate solo or in teams. If you want to participate in a team, but didn’t find your teammates yet, shoot a message on our Discord server! There will also be an opportunity to find teammates at the event itself. So what are you waiting for? Fill in the registration form and see you at the jam!

Registrations for the 2025 Game Jam are now closed. Hope to see you again next year!
