BEST Leuven is a student organisation for STEM-students that has been around for 25+ years. Our focus is to improve students on a personal and professional level.

What is BEST Leuven?

Our General Vision

We improve students on a personal and professional level by:

  • Offering our large European network of ~80 other BEST groups all over Europe to students.
  • Organising local and international events, which every STEM-student can attend.
  • Building a strong community where everyone feels welcome and excited to share ideas.

See our events page for an overview of everything we organise.

What do we offer you?

Meet students

In previous academic years, we reached almost every STEM student at KU Leuven in some way. We focus on Dutch-speaking master students but everyone is welcome at our events.

Some examples of STEM students include:

  • Industrial engineering
  • Civil engineering
  • Math, physics, biology & chemistry
  • IT/Informatics
  • Bioscience engineering
  • Etc.

Our goal is to give companies the opportunity to meet these students in a welcoming environment.

Connecting with students

Connecting with students isn’t easy. That’s why we offer you to make your brand stand out by sponsoring us. This could include:

  • Social media post (Instagram, LinkedIn, Tiktok, ...)
  • Your brand on our banner/posters/stickers/...
  • CVs/Emails of interested students
  • Opportunity to meet STEM students in a relaxed and welcoming environment at our events
  • Have an idea that’s not listed above? No worries! We are flexible and open to exploring a unique sponsoring idea. Feel free to reach out and let’s discuss!

Examples of unique sponsoring

Coding Challenge: Previous years, we designed custom challenges in our competition based around our main sponsor.

BEST Course: During one of our BEST Courses, Fuze Tea sponsored us Fuze Tea cans for everyone to enjoy.
